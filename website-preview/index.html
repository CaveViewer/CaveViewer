CaveViewer makes exploring massive 3-D maps accessible to everyone. Designed for cave divers, explorers, cartographers, and 3-D mapping enthusiasts, it uses an innovative rendering technique to display extremely large maps on anything from lightweight laptops to high-end workstations. The app is completely free—no advertisements, no subscriptions, no online accounts, and no hidden costs.

Bring a textured OBJ map with its matching MTL file. For best results, use a recent Windows, macOS, or Linux desktop with 16 GB of RAM and 256 GB of free disk space; a dedicated GPU is not required.
